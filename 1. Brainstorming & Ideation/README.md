## Introduction

PocketSmart AI is an AI-powered budgeting assistant designed to help users understand their monthly income and expenses.

The application uses Artificial Intelligence to analyze budget information and provide practical spending and savings recommendations.

## Problem Identification

Many people find it difficult to manage their monthly income and expenses.

Common problems include:

- Difficulty tracking expenses
- Unnecessary spending
- Difficulty setting savings goals
- Lack of proper budget planning
- Difficulty identifying major spending categories

## Proposed Idea

The proposed solution is PocketSmart AI.

The application allows users to enter their monthly income, expenses, and savings goal.

The system analyzes the information and provides a simple budget summary and personalized recommendations.

## AI Integration

Google Gemini AI is used to analyze the user's budget and generate useful recommendations.

## Expected Outcome

The project is expected to provide:

- Simple budget management
- Expense analysis
- Savings recommendations
- AI-powered suggestions
- User registration and login
- Database storage
- Easy-to-use web interface

## Conclusion

The brainstorming phase identified personal budget management as a useful real-world problem that can be addressed using Artificial Intelligence and web application technologies.
